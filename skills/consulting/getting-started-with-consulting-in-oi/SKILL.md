# Getting Started with Consulting and Agency Work in Ổi Browser

Consultants and agencies juggle finding clients, delivering the work, and reporting on it, often
across several clients at once. Ổi Browser works across their inbox, calendar, client tools, and
the web, and remembers each client's context.

## Setup

- What they offer, and who their best clients have been.
- How they find clients today, e.g. referrals, outreach, or inbound.
- Which parts of delivery eat the most time, e.g. proposals, reporting, or time tracking.

Find out which apps they use and connect the ones that hold this, e.g. their inbox and past
proposals.

## Things to try

- **Win new clients from signals**, e.g. companies in their area that just raised a round, posted a
  relevant job, or launched something new, each with a short one-pager on how the user could help.
  See `oi/sales/getting-started-with-sales-in-oi`.
- **Turn a client call into a proposal**: capture the meeting, then draft the offer from what was
  said and their past proposals.
- **Research a client before kickoff**, e.g. their business, market, competitors, and the people in
  the room.
- **Report progress to clients** from their project tools, analytics, and meeting notes. See
  `oi/operations/build-client-progress-report-project-tools`.
- **Time reporting**, e.g. pulling hours from calendars, meeting recordings, and project tools into
  a weekly report per client, ready to review before invoicing.
- **Turn research into a client-ready deck**. See
  `oi/research-analysis/create-beautiful-slide-deck`.

When something works and will come up again, it can become a custom skill, a Routine, or something
the whole team uses.

## Worth knowing

- Keep each client's data, context, and deliverables separate.
